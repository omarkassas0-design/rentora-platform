# Rentora demo listings

The preview storefront contains 20 fictional demo vehicles assigned to six fictional rental offices in Syrian cities. Six listings show illustrative promotional prices.

- Demo data is embedded in `src/App.tsx` and is used only when Supabase has no published, available cars (or is not configured).
- Demo prices and offers are examples, not current market quotes or real rental offers.
- Demo office names are fictional.
- Demo vehicles are not inserted into Supabase.
- Demo vehicle booking is blocked intentionally.
- When real published cars exist in Supabase, they replace the demo catalogue.

Before launch, replace demo entries with real, permissioned listings and verified office details.
